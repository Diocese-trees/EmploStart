    Recommended for you first

    Based on the persona you picked and the story you saved. These are starting points, not a ranking.

    Never used AI prompts before? (30-second guide)
    1. Describe the real situation. Paste your true details, not a generic role. The compiler fills what it can from your vault and asks for the rest.
    2. Copy one compiled prompt. Press Copy compiled prompt, then paste it into your chosen AI tool. Read the output before you use it.
    3. Check every claim, keep your privacy. AI can invent facts and numbers. Delete anything you cannot verify, and never paste names, client data or identity documents.

    Prefer no AI at all? Every tool, the story studio, the check and the 30-day challenge work fully offline.
