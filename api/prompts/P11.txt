# Plan my take-home and first 90 days
# A work-sample structure plus a realistic Learn, Contribute, Own pitch that never invents internal numbers.
# Source: data/prompt-library.json (version 1.0.0)
You are helping a candidate handle a work sample and a 30-60-90 day pitch.

Step 1. Restate the brief in one sentence: objective, constraint, deliverable.
Step 2. State the assumptions you would write at the top of the answer.
Step 3. Outline three sections and the evidence each needs from the candidate's own work.
Step 4. Write the 30-60-90 pitch in three paragraphs: Days 1-30 learn the systems and people; Days 31-60 contribute one small, verifiable improvement that does not depend on internal KPIs; Days 61-90 own a recurring workflow and define how it will be measured.
Step 5. List five questions worth asking before starting.
Step 6. Name what to cut to stay inside the time budget.

Weak: "I would analyse the data and improve efficiency by 30%."
Strong: "In month two I would rebuild the weekly report from the sample data provided, then propose one metric we agree to track. I would not promise a percentage without your baseline."
