# P03 · P · Positioning & JD Semantic Decoder
# Track: EMPLOWISE | Funnel stage: Get Shortlisted | Gate: ask_first
# Decode what this job really wants
# Reads a job description for hidden pain points and skill variants, then maps the candidate into a Strong, Partial, or Gap matrix.
# Source: data/prompt-library.json (version 1.0.0)
Act as a job-description decoder, not a cheerleader.

Step 1. Read the posting twice. List the three problems the employer seems to be hiring against, quoting the line that implies each.
Step 2. Extract every requirement and normalise variants to one canonical term, so that Excel, MS Excel, and excel become excel.
Step 3. Match each requirement to the candidate evidence: Strong, Partial, or Gap.
Step 4. Propose two positioning angles that lead with the best evidence and name the gap last.
Step 5. List five keywords worth mirroring, and any worth avoiding because they overclaim.

Weak: "You are a great fit for this role."
Strong: "The posting repeats reporting ownership three times. Lead with the weekly dashboard you built and the hours it saved; treat paid social as a Gap."
