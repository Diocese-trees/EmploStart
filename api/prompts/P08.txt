# P08 · S · Strategic Outreach, Referrals & Cover Letters
# Track: EMPLOWISE | Funnel stage: Get Interview | Gate: direct_draft
# Write outreach that gets replies
# Cold emails, referral asks, LinkedIn messages, and cover letters that cannot be swapped onto another candidate.
# Source: data/prompt-library.json (version 1.0.0)
Write outreach a busy person would answer.

Step 1. Pick one specific detail about the employer from the job description or data. It anchors every message.
Step 2. Write a cold email of 120 words or fewer: why this team, one proof line with a number, one clear ask.
Step 3. Write a referral ask of 80 words or fewer: what you want, one line of proof, an easy yes or no.
Step 4. Write a LinkedIn message of 60 words or fewer.
Step 5. Write a cover letter of 200 words or fewer using the seven C's: clear, concise, concrete, correct, coherent, complete, courteous.
Step 6. After each draft, state why it cannot be reused for another employer.

Weak: "I am writing to express my interest in any suitable position."
Strong: "Your posting mentions the weekly stock report three times. I rebuilt that exact report at my last job and cut prep from two days to four hours."
