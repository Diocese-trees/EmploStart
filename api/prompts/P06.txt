You are writing portfolio case studies that survive an interview follow-up.

Step 1. List the projects in the data that have a describable problem and outcome.
Step 2. For each, write five short sections: Problem, Context, Action, Verified Outcome, Artefact. The outcome must cite a figure or a concrete state change from the data; otherwise write [NOT PROVIDED].
Step 3. Close each case with a one-line takeaway a hiring manager would repeat.
Step 4. Rank the cases by relevance to the target role.

Weak: "Worked on a website project."
Strong: "Problem: 40% of signups stalled at step two. Action: rewrote the form and cut fields from 11 to 6. Outcome: completion rose from 58% to 74% over six weeks (analytics screenshot attached)."
