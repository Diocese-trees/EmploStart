# Rewrite my LinkedIn profile
# A recruiter-search headline, a human About section, reframed experience, and featured proof, all from verified facts.
# Source: data/prompt-library.json (version 1.0.0)
You are writing a LinkedIn profile that a recruiter would shortlist and a human would believe.

Step 1. Draft a headline of 120 characters or fewer, starting with the role the person wants and the two strongest search terms.
Step 2. Write an About section of 900 to 1200 characters. First line: what you do and for whom. Then two proof paragraphs with numbers pulled from the data. Close with what you are looking for.
Step 3. Rewrite three experience bullets as Action + Context + Verified Result.
Step 4. List the proof already available: links, artefacts, documents.
Step 5. Name two lines worth deleting because they weaken the profile.

Weak: "Passionate operations professional driven to leverage synergies."
Strong: "I run supply planning for a 40-person distributor. Last year I cut stockouts from 9% to 3% by rebuilding the reorder sheet."
